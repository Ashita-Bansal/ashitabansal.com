Moving on-prem ingestion to the cloud

Led the move of on-premises workloads onto a cloud-native, event-driven ingestion platform. Jobs that ran on on-prem VSIs now run as AWS Glue jobs orchestrated by Airflow, with Kafka carrying events between stages. Processing scales with the data, and compute costs dropped sharply.

Data pipelines

Event-driven ETL into Apache Iceberg

Designed pipelines where a new S3 file emits an EventBridge event and a Lambda starts the matching Airflow workflow. The workflow loads multi-GB raw files into Iceberg tables in under two minutes, thanks to partition pruning and Parquet vectorized reads.

RAG · LLMs · Compliance

Compliance & Document Review Platform

An AI-assisted review platform for legal and compliance teams. It reads contracts and regulatory documents, finds the clauses that matter, and checks them for compliance issues and risk.

  • Ingestion & retrieval. A pipeline chunks and embeds large document sets into a vector store, so semantic queries come back in under 150 ms.
  • Automated review. RAG over the retrieved clauses drives compliance validation and risk detection. FastAPI services backed by PostgreSQL and Redis analyze many documents at once.
  • Traceability. Observability and audit tracking record where every finding came from, which made bottlenecks easy to find.
  • Python

AI Financial Research & Market Intelligence Agent

A research agent that brings market data, earnings reports, regulatory filings and financial news into one interface for investment analysis.
